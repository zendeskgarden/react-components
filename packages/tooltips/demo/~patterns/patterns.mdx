## Toggletip

Setting the `isToggletip` prop switches the `Tooltip` into a toggletip: it opens
and closes on click rather than hover/focus, dismisses on outside click or
`Escape`, and announces its content through a live region. The trigger must be a
`<button>` element. The following example renders rich content with
`Tooltip.Title` and `Tooltip.Paragraph`.

  <Story of={Stories.Toggletip} />
</Canvas>

## Toggletip with interactive content

Because a toggletip is dismissed on click rather than blur, its content can
include interactive elements such as an `Anchor`. Focus may move into the
toggletip without closing it.

## Info toggletip

An icon-only `IconButton` is a common toggletip trigger. Because the toggletip
content is announced through a live region rather than naming the trigger, the
`IconButton` must supply its own `aria-label`.
