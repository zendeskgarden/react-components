### Accordion.Actions

<Markdown>
  {`\`Accordion.Actions\` holds header buttons that never toggle the section,
in any accordion (with or without \`isWell\`). Rendered as a direct child of
\`Accordion.Header\`, it sits outside the heading, so action labels stay out of
the heading's accessible name.

- Render it as a direct child of \`Accordion.Header\` (a fragment is fine), so
  it sits outside the heading. An \`Accordion.Actions\` nested in a consumer
  component stays inside the heading: it still never toggles, but its labels
  join the heading name (a development warning flags this).
- Keep it mounted (even empty) when its contents are conditional and \`isWell\`
  isn't set. Mounting or unmounting it switches the header layout and focus is
  lost. With \`isWell\`, the header row is always rendered, so this can't
  happen.
- In the row layout, a consumer \`onClick\` on \`Accordion.Header\` doesn't
  fire for clicks on the actions or the chevron.
- Consumer \`className\`, \`style\` and \`accordions.header\` theme overrides
  apply to the heading, not the full row. Use \`accordions.header_row\` for the
  row.
  `}
  </Markdown>
